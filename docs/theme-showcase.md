## A page for small discoveries

The best part of a notebook is the space it leaves for an unfinished thought. A sentence becomes a paragraph, a paragraph becomes a plan, and a small detail finds somewhere to stay.

Use **bold for the important part**, *italics for a quieter aside*, and ~~strikethrough for an idea that has changed~~. Keep a little `inline code` nearby when the exact wording matters.

> Leave room for the next thought.
>
> A useful note does not have to be a finished one.
